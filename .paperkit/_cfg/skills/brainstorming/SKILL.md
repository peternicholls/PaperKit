# Brainstorming

## Your Task

When facilitating brainstorming, guide the process to:

1. **Generate** many ideas without judgment
2. **Organize** ideas into meaningful categories
3. **Evaluate** ideas against criteria
4. **Select** the most promising directions
5. **Document** decisions and rationale

---

## Brainstorming Phases

### Phase 1: Divergent Thinking (Generate)

**Goal**: Maximize quantity and variety of ideas.

**Rules**:
- Defer judgment - no criticism yet
- Encourage wild ideas
- Build on others' ideas
- Stay focused on the topic
- One idea at a time
- Aim for quantity

**Techniques**:

#### Mind Mapping
Start with central concept, branch out:
```
                 [Sub-idea 1a]
                /
    [Idea 1] -- [Sub-idea 1b]
   /            \
[TOPIC]          [Sub-idea 1c]
   \
    [Idea 2] -- [Sub-idea 2a]
                \
                 [Sub-idea 2b]
```

#### SCAMPER
Apply each prompt to your topic:
- **S**ubstitute - What can be replaced?
- **C**ombine - What can be merged?
- **A**dapt - What can be adjusted?
- **M**odify - What can be changed?
- **P**ut to other uses - Other applications?
- **E**liminate - What can be removed?
- **R**everse/Rearrange - Flip or reorder?

#### Random Stimulus
Pick a random word/image, force connections to your topic.

#### Reverse Brainstorming
Ask: "How could we make this worse?" Then reverse solutions.

#### Six Thinking Hats
Rotate perspectives:
- 🎩 White: Facts only
- 🎩 Red: Emotions/intuition
- 🎩 Black: Problems/risks
- 🎩 Yellow: Benefits/optimism
- 🎩 Green: Creativity/alternatives
- 🎩 Blue: Process/meta-thinking

---

### Phase 2: Convergent Thinking (Organize)

**Goal**: Structure ideas for evaluation.

**Techniques**:

#### Affinity Grouping
1. Write each idea on a virtual sticky note
2. Silently group similar ideas
3. Name each group
4. Identify relationships between groups

#### Thematic Clustering
```markdown
## Theme: [Name]
- Idea 1
- Idea 2
- Idea 3

## Theme: [Name]
- Idea 4
- Idea 5
```

#### Priority Matrix
```
         | Low Effort | High Effort |
---------|------------|-------------|
High     | Quick Wins | Major       |
Impact   |            | Projects    |
---------|------------|-------------|
Low      | Fill-ins   | Avoid       |
Impact   |            |             |
```

---

### Phase 3: Evaluation

**Goal**: Assess ideas against criteria.

**Evaluation Criteria** (customize per project):

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Feasibility | 25% | Can we actually do this? |
| Impact | 25% | How significant is the outcome? |
| Effort | 20% | Resources required? |
| Risk | 15% | What could go wrong? |
| Alignment | 15% | Fits our goals? |

**Scoring Template**:
```markdown
| Idea | Feasibility | Impact | Effort | Risk | Align | Total |
|------|-------------|--------|--------|------|-------|-------|
| A    | 4/5         | 5/5    | 3/5    | 4/5  | 5/5   | 4.15  |
| B    | 3/5         | 4/5    | 4/5    | 3/5  | 4/5   | 3.60  |
```

---

### Phase 4: Selection

**Goal**: Choose which ideas to pursue.

**Decision Criteria**:
- Highest weighted score?
- Best fit for current constraints?
- Most innovative?
- Fastest to implement?

**Output Template**:
```markdown
## Selected Ideas

### Primary Choice: [Idea Name]
- **Score**: 4.15/5
- **Rationale**: Best balance of impact and feasibility
- **Next Steps**: [Action items]

### Backup Options:
1. [Alternative 1] - if primary fails because...
2. [Alternative 2] - if constraints change...

### Parking Lot (future consideration):
- [Idea X] - revisit when Y is resolved
- [Idea Z] - interesting but premature
```

## Session Structure

### Standard 30-Minute Session

| Time | Phase | Activity |
|------|-------|----------|
| 0-5 | Setup | Define topic, rules, goals |
| 5-15 | Generate | Divergent brainstorming |
| 15-20 | Organize | Cluster and group |
| 20-25 | Evaluate | Score top ideas |
| 25-30 | Select | Choose direction, next steps |

### Extended 60-Minute Session

| Time | Phase | Activity |
|------|-------|----------|
| 0-10 | Setup | Define topic, warm-up exercise |
| 10-25 | Generate | Multiple techniques |
| 25-35 | Organize | Affinity grouping |
| 35-45 | Evaluate | Detailed scoring |
| 45-55 | Select | Discussion, consensus |
| 55-60 | Close | Document, assign actions |

## Facilitation Tips

### Starting a Session

1. **State the challenge clearly**
   > "How might we [achieve X] while [constraint Y]?"

2. **Set expectations**
   > "We'll generate ideas for 10 minutes, then organize and evaluate."

3. **Warm up** (optional)
   > "Quick warm-up: What are 10 uses for a paperclip?"

### Keeping Momentum

- Use timers to create urgency
- Call out when energy drops
- Switch techniques if stuck
- Capture every idea, even duplicates

### Handling Blockers

| Blocker | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Premature criticism | Remind: "Save evaluation for later" |
| Dominant voice | Round-robin contributions |
| Silence | Use brainwriting (write first, share second) |
| Off-topic | Note it, redirect gently |
| Analysis paralysis | Set time limits, force decisions |

## For Academic Writing

When brainstorming for papers:

### Research Questions
- What interests you about the topic?
- What gap exists in the literature?
- What would be valuable to know?
- What's counterintuitive or surprising?

### Paper Structure
- What's the logical flow?
- What arguments support the thesis?
- What evidence is needed?
- What objections must be addressed?

### Thesis Development
- What's your core claim?
- Why does it matter?
- What's the scope?
- What's NOT included?
