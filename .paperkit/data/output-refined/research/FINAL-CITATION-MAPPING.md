## §02 Perceptual Foundations

### 2.1 Riemannian Nature of Color Space

**Claim to make:**

**Citation:** 🔴 Hong et al. (2024)

### 2.2 Impossibility of 3D Euclidean Uniformity

**Claim to make:**
> The hue circle requires 720° (not 360°) for perceptual closure, proving 3D Euclidean spaces cannot achieve perceptual uniformity.

**Citation:** 🔴 Nölle et al. (2012)

**Quote to use:**
> "'Super-importance of hue', as pointed out by Judd, requires a space for colour representations that enables unit circles to have a circumference of roughly 720°, i.e. twice the circumference of a unit circle in Euclidean space" (Nölle et al., 2012, p. 3).

**Mathematical proof to cite:**
> "The circumference of a fully saturated colour circle (S = 1) is given by: U(S=1) = ∫₀²π √g_HH dH ≈ 12.65. This value is approximately 4π" (Nölle et al., 2012, Section 3.3, Eq. 3.11).

**BibTeX:** `nolle2012`

**Where:** §2.2 or §2.3 (Topology subsection) — **critical for novelty claim**

### 2.3 Historical Foundations (Optional)

**Claim to make:**
> The Riemannian approach to color metrics has century-old foundations in Schrödinger's extension of Helmholtz's line element.

**Citation:** 🟠 Roberti & Peruzzi (2023)

**Quote to use:**
> "Schrödinger maintained a strong distinction between lower and higher color metrics. In the domain of lower color metrics, an affine space sufficed to describe the laws of color mixture. On the other hand, in the domain of higher color metrics, a Riemannian space was demonstrated to be necessary to translate color differences into color distances" (Roberti & Peruzzi, 2023, p. 319).

**BibTeX:** `roberti2023`

**Where:** §2.1 (Historical context) or Introduction (scholarly positioning)

### 7.2 Chromatic Adaptation Symmetry

**Claim to make:**
> The chromatic inversion $(L, -a, -b)$ can be conceptualized as adaptation to a complementary illuminant, with symmetry properties ensuring reversibility.

**Citation:** 🟠 Gao et al. (2020)

**Quote to use:**
> "If two stimuli s_β and s_δ are corresponding colors, then s_δ and s_β are also corresponding colors, this property being called symmetry" (Gao et al., 2020, p. 2).

**Mathematical property:**
> Symmetry: $\Gamma_{\beta,\alpha} \Gamma_{\alpha,\beta} = I_3$ (identity matrix) (Gao et al., 2020, p. 2).

**Implication to state:**
> This symmetry property guarantees that applying the Möbius inversion twice returns precisely to the origin, validating the half-twist topology.

**BibTeX:** `gao2020`

## Ready-to-Use Citation Patterns

### Pattern 1: Direct Quote

**Template:**
> As [Author(s)] ([Year]) note[s], "[exact quote with page number]" ([Author(s)], [Year], p./Section X).

**Example:**
> As Sekulovski et al. (2007) found, "the visibility threshold of smoothness is about ten times smaller for lightness changes than for chroma or hue changes" (Kong, 2021, p. 530, citing Sekulovski et al., 2007).

---

### Pattern 2: Paraphrase with Citation

**Template:**
> [Your claim in your own words] ([Author(s)], [Year]).

**Example:**
> Discrimination threshold contours are consistently oriented radially toward the achromatic center (Hong et al., 2024).

---

### Pattern 3: Multiple Citations for Same Claim

**Template:**
> [Your claim] ([Author1], [Year1]; [Author2], [Year2]).

**Example:**
> The Riemannian nature of color space has been recognized since Schrödinger's work (Roberti & Peruzzi, 2023), with modern empirical validation confirming geodesic distance frameworks (Hong et al., 2024).

---

### Pattern 4: Citing for Context (Not Evidence)

**Template:**
> As established in the psychophysics literature ([Author], [Year]), [general principle].

**Example:**
> As established in the psychophysics literature (Fechner, 1860), perceptual magnitude scales logarithmically with physical stimulus intensity.

---

### Pattern 5: Acknowledging Limitations

**Template:**
> While [validated claim] is empirically supported ([Author1], [Year1]), [design heuristic] remains a design choice requiring future validation ([Author2], [Year2]).

**Example:**
> While the 10:1 lightness-chroma asymmetry is empirically validated (Sekulovski et al., 2007), hue velocity weighting remains a design heuristic requiring future validation using 2AFC methodology (Wang et al., 2022).
